# 01 · Brand identity

Who Farsi Pen is, and how the brand shows up in the product.

## 1. Principles

- **A pen, not a game.** The name and the levels are about writing and growing literacy, from a blank page to a golden pen. The brand is calm, crafted and grown-up.
- **Persian culture, respectfully.** Colour names, poetry, ta'ârof and Nowruz come from the culture the learner is entering. They aren't used as decoration or stereotype.
- **Original.** Farsi Pen never borrows another product's logo, mascot, illustration style, fonts or exact brand colours.
- **Quiet confidence.** No mascots, no confetti, no hype. Trust comes from clarity and honesty.

### 2.3 Brand colours (roles)

Names come from Persian and the culture. Exact values are in *02 Colour*.

| Colour | Meaning of the name | Role |
|---|---|---|
| **Firuze** (فیروزه) | Turquoise, the colour of Persian tiles and domes | Primary: brand, main actions, Home |
| **Saffron** (زعفران) | Saffron | Reward: XP, stars, bookmarks, Path |
| **Anar** (انار) | Pomegranate | Error and streak flame: wrong answers, Speak |
| **Pista** (پسته) | Pistachio | Success: correct, mastered, Profile |
| **Sky** | Sky blue | Info and selection: Progress, Words, audio |
| **Plum** | Plum | Grammar, reading and exams |

Each tab has its own colour: Home firuze, Path saffron, Exams plum, Progress sky, Profile pista.

### 2.4 Personality and voice

| We are | We are not |
|---|---|
| Warm, like a patient Iranian aunt who wants you to succeed | Childish or cutesy |
| Honest ("Not yet — and that's fine") | Pushy or guilt-tripping |
| Brief and concrete | Hype ("Amazing!!! 🎉") |
| Culturally curious | Exoticising |

Full rules are in *11 Content design*.

### 2.5 Brand vocabulary

- **Level names:** pen and writing themed, in English and Persian, from Blank Page (صفحهٔ سفید) to Golden Pen (قلم طلایی).
- **Badges:** First Stroke, Warm Ink, Full Alphabet…
- **Feature names:** Today, Path, Missions, Scenes, Hands-free, My cards, Check the content.

## 3. Usage: do and don't

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Use the monogram on a firuze tile, in the token colour | Recolour the mark with gradients, or add shadows or 3D lips |
| Use colour roles consistently (saffron = reward) | Use anar for anything that isn't an error or the streak |
| Use Persian cultural references with context (a culture note) | Use calligraphy or carpet patterns as decoration |
| Keep the name "Farsi Pen" in Latin type | Write "FarsiPen", "Farsi-Pen" or "FARSI PEN" |

## 4. Accessibility notes

- The wordmark uses `firuze-ink`, which meets AA on canvas and card.
- The logo link has `aria-label="Farsi Pen home"`.
