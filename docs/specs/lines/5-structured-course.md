# Line 5 · Structured course A1 → B2

**Promise:** A full syllabus, from the first letter to reading real texts and classical poetry.

**Features in this line:** F15 Grammar course · F16 Reading track (B1) · F17 Poetry (with F02 Path as the map and F07 lessons for vocabulary)

## 1. The problem

- Phrase-based apps stall at A2. Learners can't build new sentences or read real text.
- Heritage speakers need the system behind what they already say.
- Real written Persian (kitâbi) differs from speech, and that's what news, university and SAMFA require.

## 2. Who it serves

Serious learners, heritage speakers, SAMFA candidates (B1–B2), and culture and poetry lovers.

## 3. How the features fit together

```
Path sections → A1 grammar (after Level 2) → A2 grammar (after Level 4)
             → Independent (B1): Level 5 words + 8 readings + B1 grammar
             → Confident (B2): B2 grammar + Poetry
Missions → "grammar on demand" links to the exact concept
Readings → "Grammar in the text" links back to concepts; tap any word → dictionary
```

## 4. What the prototype has today

- **Grammar:** 54 concepts in 14 units (A1 16, A2 15, B1 14, B2 9). Each has a table, points, examples and 6 exercises (147 choice, 89 fill, 88 translate in total). Pass at 80%.
- **Reading:** 8 B1 lessons × 8 steps (before reading → read → check → written vs spoken → grammar → news → culture → speak).
- **Poetry:** 12 Fâl-e Hâfez ghazals + 4 more (Saadi, Khayyam), a poem of the day, and full poems from Ganjoor in-app with explanations and tap-to-look-up.

## 5. MVP scope

1. Teacher review of every grammar explanation (162 items), then remove the "drafts" banner.
2. Native review of the 220 reading items.
3. Make the gaps clear: B2 has grammar and poetry only, with no vocabulary lessons, readings or missions.

## 6. Real application roadmap

| Now | Next | Later |
|---|---|---|
| Reviews | Spaced review of weak grammar; error-driven links from wrong answers | Conjugation tool for any verb |
| | B2 readings; graded readers; regular new readings (culture or news) | Authentic texts with permissions; read-aloud fluency scoring |
| | New exercise types (reorder, speak, transform) | Poetry path (metre, archaic words); recitations; more poets (with rights) |

## 7. Success metrics *(proposed)*

- Concept pass rate and attempts.
- Reading completion and "% understood".
- Poetry visits and fâl opens (a delight metric).
- Progression from A2 to B1 on the Path.

## 8. Risks and open questions

- Grammar written by non-teachers needs expert review before it's trusted.
- Rights for modern poets and news texts.
